Prototype render prompts
========================

These concept renders were updated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation
tool on 2026-08-05. Mode: precise-object-edit of the 2026-08-04 two-button
renders. They communicate design intent and are not evidence of engineering or
manufacture.

The hero first received a control-hierarchy edit. A targeted follow-up changed
the oversized round Next cap into the final restrained rounded pill. The final
hero was then supplied as the exterior-design reference for the tabletop and
exploded views.
