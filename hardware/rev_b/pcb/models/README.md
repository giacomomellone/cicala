# Component envelopes

These original, simplified STEP models restore the missing component
bodies in KiCad. The inherited models are generated by `../../../tools/pcb/make_component_models.py`
with CadQuery and use CERN-OHL-S-2.0, like the project hardware. They are
nominal envelopes with illustrative connector cavities; they are not supplier
models and do not establish assembled fit. No third-party CAD is redistributed.

Models use millimetres, footprint origin, model Y = negative footprint Y, and
positive Z above the mounting surface. Board-side transforms are applied by
KiCad. Open the project to resolve `${KIPRJMOD}/models/...` paths.

The PCB sits beside the protected pack. The case includes separate mated-JST
and wire-corridor reserves, and the USB opening admits a nominal 12 × 4 mm
overmold. The display's 180° flex loop reaches the top-side connector.
`hardware/tools/case/export_component_bounds.py` derives fitted component boxes from
the STEP export and fingerprints PCB placement and local model files. Capacitors use a 1.5 mm envelope, except the selected 1206 GRM31 parts at their 1.8 mm maximum.
Qualify actual mating parts, wire bends and print tolerances on prototypes.

## Rev B connector and switch envelopes

`Alps_SKRABCE010_envelope.step` reserves a 6.2 × 6.2 mm body and a 3.65 mm maximum stem height. `Molex_503480-2400_envelope.step` reserves a 13.7 × 4 × 1.2 mm connector body. These are original dimensional envelopes, generated by `hardware/tools/rev_b/make_switch_model.py`, not supplier CAD. Sources: [Alps SKRABCE010](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/e/products/detail/SKRABCE010/) and [Molex 503480-2400](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/5034802400). The standard KiCad JST SH model supplies J3.
