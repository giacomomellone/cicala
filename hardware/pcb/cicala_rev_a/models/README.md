# Component envelopes

These original, simplified STEP models restore the six missing component
bodies in KiCad. They are generated by `../../tools/make_component_models.py`
with CadQuery and use CERN-OHL-S-2.0, like the project hardware. They are
nominal envelopes with illustrative connector cavities; they are not supplier
models and do not establish assembled fit. No third-party CAD is redistributed.

Models use millimetres, footprint origin, model Y = negative footprint Y, and
positive Z above the mounting surface. Board-side transforms are applied by
KiCad. Open the project to resolve `${KIPRJMOD}/models/...` paths.

The PCB sits above the protected pack. The case includes separate mated-JST
and wire-corridor reserves, and the USB opening admits a nominal 12 × 6 mm
overmold. The display's 180° flex loop reaches the top-side connector.
`hardware/case/export_component_bounds.py` derives fitted component boxes from
the STEP export and fingerprints PCB placement and local model files. Generic
capacitor models are conservatively bounded at 1.5 mm mounted height.
Qualify actual mating parts, wire bends and print tolerances on prototypes.
